# Draft city questions — not sent

Purpose: clarify access to existing data for community civic projects. Review the relevant source entry and remove questions already answered before contacting a department.

- Who maintains this dataset, and what published contact should receive corrections?
- Is an existing CSV, spreadsheet, geographic download or read-only API available?
- What fields and historical periods are included? Is a dictionary available?
- How frequently is it updated, and how can consumers identify its effective date or version?
- Does access or a bulk export have a fee?
- What terms govern reuse and redistribution?
- Are there known omissions, revisions or differences between public lookup and internal records?

For wards, ask which boundary plan is current. For assessments, ask which valuation/Grand List date the available records represent. For finance, distinguish reports, appropriations, payments and procurement records.

These are draft access questions, not a submitted public-records request. No outreach has been sent.
